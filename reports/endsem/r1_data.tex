\section{DL data, splits and regularisation}
We reuse 720 windows from MH\_01\_easy and 592 from MH\_02\_easy.
Each contains 400 samples in six inertial channels. The two leave-one-flight-out
folds retain the final $\lceil0.2N\rceil$ source windows for validation.
Training intervals satisfy $\max(t_{end})+4\,s\leq\min(t_{validation,start})$.
The resulting training/validation/test sizes are 450/119/720 and 553/144/592;
23 candidate windows are purged in either fold.

Means and population standard deviations are fitted only to inner training.
The same saved statistics transform validation and test inputs. Physics bias is
calibrated from the union of inner-training sensor intervals, with matched
ground-truth timestamps also restricted to those intervals. Original Phase 1
physics files remain separate. Labels and physics still use ground-truth initial
states; this is an offline endpoint-error experiment, not autonomous navigation.

The shared Dataset returns channels-first IMU, 104 engineered features, four
physics values, magnitude and vector labels, and original window metadata.
Optional camera inputs are three 64-by-64 grayscale frames scaled to $[0,1]$.
Actual camera arrays await R3. Camera-off omits the key entirely.

Augmentation affects training observations only. Clipped noise is bounded below
sensor noise; edge crop-and-mask retains the full labelled interval. It does not
represent a shorter physical trajectory. Targets and reference feature modalities
remain unchanged. Time warping, amplitude scaling and target-changing crops are
excluded. Task-dependent augmentation motivates this restriction
\cite{iwana2021,wen2021}. Dropout and weight decay require trainer integration
\cite{srivastava2014,loshchilov2019}.

A1 prepares eight factorial combinations of dropout, decay and early stopping,
plus three augmentation probes. A2 prepares nested 50, 75 and 100 percent prefixes
of eligible inner training, with independent scalers and physics calibration.
Validation and test remain fixed. There are 84 jobs across two folds and three
seeds. No model experiments have been run; accuracy comparisons remain pending.
A2 confounds chronological coverage with quantity and must be interpreted accordingly.
